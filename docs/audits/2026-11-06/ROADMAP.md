## Work sequence and safe parallelism

1. **Baseline and containment.** Preserve the evidence fixtures, establish full CI discovery, prepare the dependency patch and restrict unsafe personalized picks. A source-data contract change and a dependency-only change can be developed independently. Review framework/CI lockfile interactions together before integration. While availability evidence is missing, offer a dated last-publication view and generic logging.
2. **Correct the two records students rely on.** Dining lane: [F02](FINDINGS.md#f02) → [F10](FINDINGS.md#f10)/[F12](FINDINGS.md#f12) → [F09](FINDINGS.md#f09)/[F11](FINDINGS.md#f11)/[F18](FINDINGS.md#f18). Logging lane: [F05](FINDINGS.md#f05) and [F04](FINDINGS.md#f04) → [F06](FINDINGS.md#f06); [F07](FINDINGS.md#f07) is a small independent route correction. Agree on the distinction between food, offering and logged line before the dining and logging lanes both edit snapshot types. Keep P0 patches small; move schema consolidation to the next release.
3. **Protect accumulated data.** [F08](FINDINGS.md#f08)/[F14](FINDINGS.md#f14)/[F19](FINDINGS.md#f19) → [F15](FINDINGS.md#f15)/[F20](FINDINGS.md#f20). Exercise legacy export/restore, device clearing, duplicate actions and failure recovery before any data migration. Then align product context and sensitive opt-in ([F16](FINDINGS.md#f16)/[F22](FINDINGS.md#f22)), and keyboard/assistive-tech navigation ([F21](FINDINGS.md#f21)). Local-only scope disclosure can ship earlier without storage changes.
4. **Reduce operational and engineering cost.** Once the same-date contract is stable, measure and reduce extra reads ([F17](FINDINGS.md#f17)/[F23](FINDINGS.md#f23)); update source health ([F18](FINDINGS.md#f18)). Compare and retire superseded code/docs ([F24](FINDINGS.md#f24)/[F25](FINDINGS.md#f25)) separately from feature fixes. Run the scoring audit ([F26](FINDINGS.md#f26)) only on comparable data; tuning cannot fix missing coverage.
5. **Product pilots.** Repeat meals and trustworthy discovery ([F27](FINDINGS.md#f27)) first. Try a coverage-aware weekly view ([F28](FINDINGS.md#f28)) after totals are dependable. [F29](FINDINGS.md#f29)/[F30](FINDINGS.md#f30)/[F31](FINDINGS.md#f31) require owner decisions or real demand. Evaluate by task completion: can a student find an appropriate available lunch, plan it for the right day, log yesterday's meal, and recover their data? Do not infer engagement from a higher recommendation score.

**Quick wins:** Market intent/date regression; portable test discovery; hide Going Out discovery until opt-in; per-item demo labeling; missing edit-target error; generic photo labels; remove the one unused-parameter lint warning. Correct labels without claiming improved data coverage.

**Best long-term investments:** authorized, versioned dining publications with explicit coverage; stable food identity separate from offerings; a single immutable meal snapshot and arithmetic model; consistent recommendation preparation; a tested, recoverable student data boundary.

**Combine deliberately:** coverage types with allergen eligibility; date contract with date-aware ranking/display; totals with estimate quality; plan validation with import rollback; operator receipts with recovery diagnostics. **Keep separate:** framework update vs food schema migration; safe guest improvements vs cloud accounts; cleanup/dead-code removals vs regressions in active UI.

**Potentially destructive work:** deleting orphaned history/feedback, merging food identities, replacing browser stores, uploading guest records, pruning branches or replacing the only good publication. First generate a read-only diff/count including ambiguous cases, export the original, verify round-trip reconciliation on copies, ship a dual reader or rollback switch, and retain the old revision. Require a deliberate owner/user decision for bulk migration, remote provisioning, closure or deletion. This audit did none of these.

**Do not pursue now:** merging the large historical gamification/theme PRs wholesale; retrying blocked feeds with new fingerprints; guessing allergen absence; reconstructing historical portions from a current menu; rewriting the entire frontend for style; standing up a student database for the current guest flow; building a native wrapper, new campus integration or university dashboard without a validated use case and data rights.
